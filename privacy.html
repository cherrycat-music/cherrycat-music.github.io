Privacy Policy — Cherry Cat Uploader

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Cherry Cat Uploader ("the App") is an internal tool used only by the owner of the Cherry Cat YouTube channel (https://www.youtube.com/@CherryCat-CC) to upload the channel's own music videos to that same channel. The App is not offered to any other users.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies.

Information the App accesses

  • With the owner's permission (OAuth), the App uploads videos, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails to the owner's own YouTube channel, and reads basic information about that channel and its videos (for example, the upload and processing status).
  • The App does not access, collect or store information about any other YouTube users or viewers.

How information is stored and shared

  • The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is used only to perform the uploads above.
  • The App does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • The App does not use cookies or tracking, and has no advertising.

Data retention and deletion

  • The App keeps only the IDs of the owner's own uploaded videos and their publish times, in a local database on the owner's computer.
  • No data from YouTube API Services is stored on any server, and nothing is kept longer than needed to manage the owner's own uploads.
  • To have this data deleted, revoke access (below) or contact us at the address below; we delete the stored token and video IDs within 7 days.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page. When access is revoked, the stored token stops working; the owner can also delete it from the computer.
